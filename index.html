How to play

  • 🎯
    Eat the food Every red dot makes you longer and adds to your score.
  • ⌨️
    Steer Arrow keys or WASD — no reversing allowed.
  • 💀
    Don't crash Hit a wall or your own tail and it's game over.
  • ⚡
    Speed increases Every few points the snake accelerates — stay sharp!
  • ⏸
    Pause anytime Press P or Esc to freeze the action.
